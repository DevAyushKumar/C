What are operators ?
-> Operators in C language is a symbol that is used to perform mathematical operations.

types:
1. Arithmetic operation:
-> They are like your school math.
symbol: +,-,*,/,%

2. Relational operators:
-> It is used to compare two values
Note:
The result is always either true(1) or false(0)
Operators:-
a) ==
b) !=
c) >
d) <
e) >=
f) <=

3. logical operators:
-> Used to combine conditions in decision making. 
Operators:-
a) && (logical AND)
b) || (logical OR)
c) ! (logical NOT)

4. Assignment operator:
-> It is used to assign values to variables.
Operators:-
a) =-
b) +=
c) -=
d) *=
e) /=

5. Ternary operator:
-> It is alternate of if-else.
Operator:-
condition ? expression1 : expression2

6. Increment and decrement operators:
These increate or decreate a value by 1.

7. Bitwise operators:
used to perform bit-level operation (0 and 1)
operator:
a) & (AND)
b) | (OR)
c) ^ (XOR)
d) ~ (NOT)
e) << (left shift)
f) >> (right shift)

8. Mischellaneous operator:
Other special operator.
Opertaor:
a) sizeof
b) ' (comma)
c) & (address of operator)
d) * (value at address operator)

Operators precedence and associativity:
Operators precedence:
-> It determines the order in which operators are evaluated in an expression. Operators with higher precedence are evaluated first.

Note:
Multiplication (*) has higher precedence than addition (+)

operator assoviativity:
-> It defines a direction (left to right) or (right to left). When same precedure operators come.

Note:
Division (|) and multiplication (*) have same precedence  (left to right)
